04 · Product education

What this protocol actually does.

Read before you check out — these are the things our physicians want every member to understand before starting.

Who it's for

Best candidates

Men with mild-to-moderate androgenetic alopecia (Norwood I–IV) who have at least 18 months of available follicles.

  • Visible miniaturization at the crown or temples
  • No history of finasteride sensitivity
  • Realistic expectation of slowed loss + partial regrowth
How it works

Mechanism, plainly

Finasteride blocks DHT conversion at the follicle; minoxidil extends anagen phase and increases follicular blood flow.

  • 5α-reductase type II inhibition (oral)
  • Vasodilation + K+ channel opening (topical)
  • Complementary pathways, additive in trial data
What to expect

Realistic timeline

Initial shedding is common in months 1–3. Visible density changes typically begin around month 6 with continued use.

  • Months 1–3 — shedding phase, expected
  • Months 4–6 — early density stabilization
  • Months 6–12 — measurable regrowth in responders
Safety considerations

Known tradeoffs

The molecules are well-characterized; their side-effect profile is too. Your physician will discuss these specifically.

  • Possible sexual side effects (~2–4% in trials)
  • Topical irritation or scalp dryness
  • Not for use during pregnancy or nursing
When this isn't right

Decline criteria

Adonis providers will decline approval when this protocol is not appropriate. We'd rather say no than ship the wrong thing.

  • Pregnancy, nursing, or planning conception
  • History of finasteride-related side effects
  • Non-androgenic causes (alopecia areata, scarring, telogen)

05 · Before you check out

Direct answers, before you order.

How does provider review actually work?
After checkout you'll complete a medical questionnaire built for this specific protocol. A Florida-licensed physician reads your answers, your medication list, and any flags — then either approves, requests follow-ups, modifies the prescription, or declines. You are never auto-prescribed. Average review time is 18 hours; expedited review is available.
What happens immediately after checkout?
You'll receive an email with a link to your medical questionnaire and your member portal. We hold the full charge until your provider has cleared the case. If your case is declined, your card is refunded automatically.
What if I'm not approved?
If a provider determines the protocol isn't appropriate for you, you'll receive a written explanation and a full refund. In some cases the provider will suggest an alternative protocol or recommend the Precision Care path. There is no charge for a declined case.
How do subscriptions work?
Subscriptions ship on the cadence you select (monthly or bi-monthly). You can pause, modify dose, or cancel from your member portal — no call, no retention pitch. Subscription members are eligible for quarterly check-ins with their provider, included.
When does the medication ship?
Medication ships only after provider approval. Typical timeline is 3–5 business days from approval to your door for generics, 5–7 for compounded protocols. All shipments are unbranded with tracking and signature on request.
Are results guaranteed?
No. No reputable medical practice can guarantee a clinical outcome, and we won't either. Response varies based on biology, genetics, baseline severity, and adherence. We track outcomes longitudinally and adjust your protocol when the data calls for it.

Notes · WordPress + WooCommerce Reusable across treatment products Map to existing post types & ACF fields

Built on WooCommerce, without looking like it.

Every section above is a reusable component. Below is how each one maps to native WordPress + WooCommerce primitives, with notes on where ACF / custom fields are needed.

1 · Product hero WC core + ACF

  • Title — WC post_title
  • Short clinical statement — acf:positioning (rich text, ~40 words)
  • Starting price — WC _price from variations, lowest active
  • One-time / bi-monthly — WooCommerce Subscriptions plugin, two variations sharing parent product
  • Vial illustration — SVG in acf:hero_illustration or featured image w/ overlay
  • Reassurance copy — global acf:reassurance_text (theme-options, not per-product)

2 · Selection module WC variations

  • Dose options — pa_dose attribute, term per option
  • Cadence — pa_cadence attribute · "one-time" + "bi-monthly"
  • Subscription pricing — Subscriptions plugin, sign-up fee = $0, billing = every 2 months
  • "What's included" — acf:included_items (repeater, 3–6 rows)
  • Save-percent badge — calculated server-side, not hard-coded

3 · How it works theme-options

  • Five steps are shared across all products → store in theme options, not per-product
  • Override per-product via acf:flow_override if a treatment has a non-standard step
  • Render with a single template part: template-parts/product-flow.php

4 · Clinical credibility CPT: physicians

  • Physicians are a custom post type (physician) with name, role, license, quote, image
  • Each product has acf:reviewing_physician (post-object field → physician post)
  • Compliance row — global theme options (HIPAA, 503A/B, FL licensure, etc.)
  • Lead paragraph — acf:credibility_lead (overrideable per product, e.g. "hair transplant surgical training" line)

5 · Product education 5 ACF blocks

  • Five fixed slots: who-its-for, how-it-works, what-to-expect, safety, decline-criteria
  • Each is a flexible-content block: { paragraph, bullet_list }
  • Decline-criteria block uses warn CSS modifier (orange tag)
  • If a slot is empty → render skipped, never blank card

6 · Express vs Precision upsell shared partial

  • Static across all products — single partials/upsell-precision.php
  • Toggleable per product via acf:show_precision_upsell (default: true)
  • Link target = a single CMS page (/precision), not a product
  • Precision is not a WooCommerce product — it's a service intake routed via Gravity Forms or similar

7 · FAQ CPT: faq + taxonomy

  • FAQ is a custom post type tagged by faq_topic taxonomy
  • Six questions per page are pulled by topic (e.g. tax = checkout-flow)
  • Per-product overrides via acf:additional_faqs (relationship field)
  • Native <details>/<summary> — accessible without JS

8 · Disclaimer + footer theme-options

  • Disclaimer text is global, not per-product — theme-options:medical_disclaimer
  • State licensure line auto-populated from theme-options:licensed_states
  • Compliance footer (HIPAA, BAA, 503A, license #) — single source of truth

Order flow WC + intake

  • WC checkout completes → order status: on-hold (not processing)
  • Order action triggers email with intake link (Gravity Forms / ManyContacts / custom)
  • Card is authorized only at checkout, captured on provider approval
  • Provider review system is external — Adonis does not build a custom prescribing platform; integrate Prescribery, MDLive partner, or comparable
  • On approval webhook → order moves to processing, pharmacy fulfills
  • On decline webhook → order moves to refunded, automatic

Compliance constraints do-not

  • Never imply automatic approval — every CTA pairs with provider-review reassurance
  • Never imply guaranteed results — FAQ answers explicitly deny
  • Never imply instant prescriptions — flow shows ~18hr review window
  • Do not duplicate Prescribery's clinical workflow — defer to integration partner for the medical layer
  • State availability gated server-side via shipping address; UI shows "FL only" badge while expanding

Design tokens shared

  • Inherited from Adonis design system v4.5 — graphite + warm ivory + bronze
  • Secondary green (--sec: #2E4338) reserved for upsell + clinical-layer accents only
  • One CTA color (--acc: #C9A35E) — never multiple gradient buttons
  • Editorial italic (Fraunces) used for emphasis only, never as body copy

Reusability per product fields needed

  • acf:positioning — 1 paragraph
  • acf:dose_options — repeater (3 rows × { label, sublabel, formula })
  • acf:reviewing_physician — post-object
  • acf:credibility_lead — 1 paragraph
  • acf:education_blocks — flexible content (5 slots)
  • acf:additional_faqs — relationship
  • Everything else is global / shared.
