Is Adonis a real medical practice or a marketplace?
We are a registered Florida telehealth practice. Every prescription is reviewed and signed by one of our staff physicians, and our pharmacy partners are FDA-registered 503A/B facilities. We are not a marketplace that forwards your information to third-party prescribers.
Florida #PA-198142HIPAA · BAA-covered
Do I need labs to start?
No. Express members can begin with a medical intake alone if their history is uncomplicated. Labs are required for the Precision path and recommended (but not required) any time you're starting a hormone-affecting protocol.
How is my prescription actually decided?
A physician reads your intake, your labs (if you opted in), and your pharmacogenomic results (Precision path), then chooses molecule, dose, and form. There is no algorithm that auto-dispenses based on quiz answers.
Can I keep my outside physician in the loop?
Yes — and we recommend it. Members can request a written care summary at any time, sent directly to their outside physician. The Lab Intelligence path is built for this kind of co-management.
What does it cost, and does insurance apply?
Express protocols start at $39/month, Precision at $129/month including the first lab panel. We don't bill insurance — but HSA and FSA cards are accepted, and most members find total cost well below copay-and-doctor visit pricing.
What happens if I want to stop?
You can pause or cancel from your member portal at any time — no call, no retention pitch. Unused doses are yours to keep. We'll send a final care summary on request.
