05 · About the AI

Pattern recognition, not a diagnosis.

The intelligence in Lab Intelligence is in how the data is organized, summarized, and made legible — not in any attempt to replace your physician. We're explicit about both sides.

What it does

Organize. Summarize.
Help you ask better questions.

  • AI-powered organization of your lab data — sorting markers across hormones, thyroid, metabolic, inflammation, nutrients, and cardiometabolic categories.
  • Pattern recognition across panels — flags markers that have moved meaningfully between draws, or that sit outside reference ranges.
  • Educational summaries in plain English — what each marker generally indicates, where reference ranges come from, and why a physician might want to look closer.
  • Helps you prepare better questions for your provider. Saves a one-page discussion summary you can share with any physician — yours, ours, or another.
Educational use. Reviewed against published reference ranges. Not personalized medical advice.
What it does not do

Doesn't diagnose. Doesn't
prescribe. Doesn't replace.

  • Does not diagnose — no condition labels, no "you have X." Reference comparisons only, with the explicit framing that diagnosis requires a licensed provider.
  • Does not prescribe — no medication recommendations, no dosing, no protocols. Prescriptions are issued only by a licensed provider via Precision Care.
  • Does not deliver a treatment plan — pattern flags are educational. A clinical plan requires intake, history, and physician judgment.
  • Does not replace your physician — and we don't market it as one. No "AI doctor", no "AI treatment plan", no guarantees of optimization. Your provider is your provider.
Banned framings. AI doctor · AI diagnosis · AI prescription · AI treatment plan · guaranteed optimization. Not used here, not used in marketing.

09 · Common questions

Things worth asking.

If you don't see your question here, member services replies the same business day during launch. We answer in plain language.

Q · 01Can I upload outside labs?

Yes. PDFs, photos, and CSV exports from Quest, LabCorp, your last physical, or other CLIA-certified labs are all supported. We organize the data; the original file remains yours and is downloadable from your member portal at any time.

Q · 02Can I order labs through Adonis?

Yes — single-category panels start at $199, full panels at $349. We use the same Quest / LabCorp partner network as Precision Care, with at-home phlebotomy as an option. Lab spend credits toward the $499 Precision Care package if you upgrade within 90 days.

Q · 03Is this medical advice?

No. Lab Intelligence is for educational review and organization of health data. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. For clinical interpretation and personalized medical guidance, upgrade to Precision Care or share your insights summary with your existing provider.

Q · 04Can AI prescribe treatment?

No. No AI on this platform — or any other reputable platform — can prescribe medications. Prescriptions are issued only by a licensed physician, only after reviewing your intake, history, and current presentation, only when medically appropriate. We're explicit about this throughout the product.

Q · 05When should I upgrade to Precision Care?

Upgrade when you want a physician's interpretation of your data — whether because something on your panel concerns you, because you have symptoms you'd like correlated to your bloodwork, or because you want a written plan. Lab Intelligence is the on-ramp; Precision Care is where labs become a plan.

Members commonly upgrade after seeing 1–2 flagged markers, or after a follow-up draw shows movement they'd like to discuss with a clinician.

Q · 06Are my labs secure?

Documents are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), stored in a HIPAA-compliant environment, accessible only to you. We don't sell, share, or use your data for advertising. You can request deletion of any uploaded document at any time from the member portal.

Q · 07Can I use this for hormones, thyroid, nutrients, and longevity?

Yes — those are exactly the categories Lab Intelligence is built around. The six default categories are hormones, thyroid, metabolic, inflammation, nutrients, and cardiometabolic risk. If your panel includes markers in these areas, they'll be organized and summarized educationally. Clinical interpretation still requires Precision Care.

Notes · WordPress + WooCommerce + Prescribery + AI guardrails Phased build · upload v1 · panel orders v1 · AI insights v2

A phased on-ramp. Upload v1, AI insights v2, upgrade-bridge always.

Lab Intelligence is built as a phased product so we can ship a useful, compliance-safe v1 (upload + organize + bridge to Precision Care) without taking dependencies on AI lab interpretation that don't exist yet. The page is the same in v1 and v2 — only the insight depth grows.

Page architecture WP page template

  • Slug /lab-intelligence — custom page template page-lab-intelligence.php
  • Composed of 11 reusable Gutenberg blocks mirroring Express + Precision pages
  • Marketing-controlled copy via ACF: hero, eduline (compliance disclosure), price anchors, FAQ list, AI does/doesn't lists
  • Single page, mobile-first; layout adapts via the same v4.5 design tokens

Reusable blocks Gutenberg

  • adonis/hero-pathway reused with educational eduline slot variant
  • adonis/path-tri · 3-up pathway selector (Upload · Order · Upgrade) — new block
  • adonis/insights-preview · marketing visualization, takes a static JSON of categories — not a live data view
  • adonis/how-it-works · reused, supports a bridge step variant
  • adonis/ai-positioning · does/doesn't pair card — new compliance-critical block
  • adonis/upgrade-bridge · bordered callout block, links to Precision Care
  • adonis/compare-cards · reused (Lab Intel · Precision Care variant)
  • adonis/trust-grid · reused
  • adonis/faq-accordion · adonis/disclaimer · adonis/final-cta reused

WooCommerce products two SKUs at launch

  • ADX-LI-UPLOAD — free product, simple, virtual. Adds an "intake" line item to cart with $0 — used to track funnel + ToS acceptance
  • ADX-LI-PANEL — variable product, simple, virtual. Variations: hormone-only ($199), thyroid+metabolic ($249), full panel ($349). At-home phlebotomy add-on as a variation attribute
  • Stripe capture_method: manual on paid panels — captured after kit shipment
  • Lab credit: a 90-day coupon code is generated on every paid panel order, stamped against the customer email, redeemable on ADX-PRC-01 Precision Care

Upload pipeline HIPAA-safe

  • WP custom endpoint /wp-json/adonis/v1/labs/upload · WP nonce + auth required
  • Uploads stream to S3 (HIPAA-eligible region) with object-level encryption, server-side AES-256
  • WordPress stores only the S3 key + filename + filetype on a wp_adonis_lab_uploads meta table — not the file itself
  • Member-initiated deletion: deletes object + soft-deletes row + writes audit log
  • Supported MIME: application/pdf, image/jpeg, image/png, text/csv; max 25MB per file
  • Virus scan via ClamAV before the file is acknowledged in the UI

Prescribery integration defer where possible

  • Lab ordering goes through Prescribery — POST /lab-orders with panel SKU and member ID
  • Prescribery returns requisition_url + kit_tracking
  • Webhook lab.results_available → results land in Prescribery's clinical store; an event is mirrored to Adonis with metadata only (panel, draw date, result count)
  • Adonis does not ingest raw lab values in v1 — the educational preview uses the member's uploaded file as the source
  • If member upgrades to Precision Care, the lab order ID is passed into the case; physician sees results natively in Prescribery

AI positioning compliance-critical

  • v1 (launch): no AI lab interpretation. Insights are deterministic — file is parsed, markers categorized into the six buckets, compared against published reference ranges, summarized with hand-written copy. The "AI" framing is restricted to organization and pattern flagging
  • v2 (future): AI summaries via a separate Prescribery-or-equivalent integration with BAA. Until that BAA is signed, no PHI flows into a third-party LLM
  • Banned framings — must never appear: "AI doctor", "AI diagnosis", "AI treatment plan", "AI prescription", "guaranteed optimization"
  • Required pairing — every mention of AI on the page is paired with the does/doesn't card or the eduline disclosure

Upgrade bridge cart logic

  • Member clicks "Upgrade to Precision Care" → adds ADX-PRC-01 to cart
  • If member has a paid panel order <90 days old, the lab-credit coupon auto-applies in the cart (woocommerce_before_calculate_totals filter)
  • Uploaded labs are passed forward as a meta link on the new order, accessible to the physician inside Prescribery's case view
  • Same Prescribery handoff hook fires as on a vanilla Precision Care order — no special branch

Member portal uploads + summaries

  • WC My Account adds a Lab uploads tab — list of uploaded files, parse status, delete action
  • Insights tab shows the same categorical view as the marketing preview, but populated from the member's actual uploaded file (deterministic v1)
  • Trends tab activates when ≥2 panels exist for the same member · same marker (line chart, identical to marketing trend)
  • Save / share: insights export as a one-page discussion summary PDF — explicit "Educational. Bring this to your provider." footer baked in
  • No AI chat, no clinical questions, no diagnostic claims anywhere in the portal copy

Compliance copy required & banned

  • Required on the hero, the upload affordance, the insights preview, the AI section, and the disclaimer: educational-only framing
  • Required in disclaimer, exact phrasing: "Lab Intelligence is designed for educational review and organization of health data. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or replace medical care…"
  • Banned: "diagnose", "treat", "prescribe", "cure", "guaranteed", "AI doctor" in marketing or product surfaces
  • Banned: framing any specific marker movement as a diagnosis or as causally indicating a condition

Do-not-build defer to Prescribery / future

  • No custom AI medical platform — all third-party LLM calls require BAA + signed risk review before launch
  • No duplicate of Prescribery's lab management — Adonis does not store raw lab values in v1
  • No custom physician dashboard — Prescribery owns the clinician UI
  • No custom diagnosis engine, no condition labeling, no risk-score outputs
  • No push notifications around lab values without provider review attached

Phased rollout v1 → v2

  • v1 (launch): Upload pipeline · panel orders · deterministic insights · upgrade bridge · trust + AI positioning · FAQ + disclaimer
  • v1.1: trends across multiple uploads · discussion summary PDF export · lab-credit coupon flow tested end-to-end
  • v2 (post-BAA): AI-enhanced summaries · category-level pattern flags · richer marker descriptions — gated behind explicit "AI use" toggle in member preferences
  • Marketing page does not change between v1 and v2 — copy is written for v2 capabilities and back-compatible with v1's deterministic output

Design tokens shared with Express + Precision

  • Same v4.5 dark-luxury system — no Lab-Intelligence-only color
  • What signals "educational" here, not "clinical": eduline disclosure callout in hero · "Example preview" / "Educational view" tags on every visualization · 3-card pathway selector (vs Precision's package card peak)
  • What signals "upgrade-ready": single bronze-bordered surface — the upgrade bridge — same visual peak pattern as Precision's pricing card, repurposed for the bridge
  • Shared partials: parts/notice-strip · parts/footer · parts/disclaimer · parts/eduline (new)
