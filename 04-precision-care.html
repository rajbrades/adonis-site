09 · Common questions

Things worth asking.

If you don't see your question here, member services replies the same business day during launch. We answer in plain language, not legalese.

Q · 01Is Precision Care required for treatment?

No. Express Care is the right path for most members seeking treatment for pattern hair loss or ED. Precision Care is for members who want lab-level context — hormones, thyroid, metabolic markers — alongside, before, or instead of treatment.

Many members start with Express Care, see good results, and only move to Precision Care if questions remain or new concerns arise.

Q · 02Can I start with Express Care first?

Yes. Express Care is a separate pathway with its own questionnaire, provider review, and treatment options. You can move to Precision Care at any time — it's the deeper diagnostic complement, not a prerequisite or a replacement.

Q · 03What labs are included?

The standard Precision Care panel covers ~50 biomarkers across six categories: hormones (testosterone total + free, SHBG, estradiol, DHT, prolactin, cortisol), full thyroid, metabolic / insulin axis, inflammation, nutrients, and cardiometabolic risk (ApoB, Lp(a), particle sizing).

Your physician may request additional or alternate markers based on your intake. Specific markers and reference ranges depend on your case.

Q · 04What happens after I purchase?

Your card is authorized but not charged in full at checkout. A member-services coordinator confirms your eligibility, schedules your physician visit, and ships your phlebotomy kit (or schedules a LabCorp draw). Charge is captured at that confirmation. No charge if you don't qualify clinically.

Q · 05Can I use outside labs?

If you've had compatible labs run within the last 90 days, you can submit them and we'll discount the package by the lab portion (typically $150). Your physician decides whether the panels are sufficient or if any markers need to be re-drawn.

Q · 06Does this guarantee a prescription?

No. Precision Care provides medical evaluation and personalized recommendations. A prescription is issued only when medically appropriate, by a licensed physician, after reviewing your labs, intake, and visit. No reputable medical practice can guarantee a prescription, and we don't.

Q · 07Is this available for men and women?

Adonis is currently focused on men's performance medicine — hair, sexual wellness, hormone, thyroid, and metabolic optimization. A women's pathway with its own panels, plans, and physicians is in active development; join the early access list from the women's home view.

Medical disclaimer

Precision Care provides lab-guided medical evaluation and personalized recommendations. Prescriptions or therapies are provided only when medically appropriate after review by a licensed provider. Results vary.

Adonis is not a substitute for emergency care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number. The information on this page is for general educational purposes; it is not personalized medical advice.

Notes · WordPress + WooCommerce + ACF + Prescribery WC product type · reusable blocks · do-not-build

Built on WooCommerce + Prescribery, sold like a service.

Precision Care is a single WooCommerce simple product ($499) wrapped in a marketing landing page. The clinical workflow — intake, scheduling, video visit, plan delivery — is delegated to Prescribery and a small set of integration hooks. Adonis owns the page, not the prescribing system.

Page architecture WP page template

  • Slug /precision-care — custom page template page-precision-care.php
  • Composed of 11 reusable Gutenberg blocks, one per section — see "Reusable section blocks" below
  • Mobile is the same page; layout adapts via the CSS in this design — no separate mobile theme
  • Content fields driven by ACF so marketing can edit copy without dev — eyebrow, h2, body, CTA labels, price anchor, panel name

WooCommerce product simple, virtual

  • Product type: simple · virtual ✓ · downloadable ✗
  • SKU ADX-PRC-01 · regular price $499 · tax class · service
  • Not a subscription — Precision Care is a one-time package. Treatment Rx, if recommended, is sold as a separate subscription product after the visit.
  • Stock: managed manually based on physician availability; flip to "out of stock" when the visit calendar is saturated
  • Eligibility: billing_state == FL required at checkout — server-side check, not just client validation

Reusable section blocks Gutenberg

  • adonis/hero-pathway — h1 + subhead + price anchor + dual CTA + diagnostic visual slot
  • adonis/included-grid — 4-up "what's in the package" with glyph + tag
  • adonis/who-its-for — numbered row list, configurable rows
  • adonis/biomarker-panel — sample-panel visual, takes a JSON of categories
  • adonis/how-it-works — vertical steps with stamps
  • adonis/compare-cards — Express vs Precision side-by-side
  • adonis/docs-credibility — physician card + cred list
  • adonis/package-card — pricing card bound to a WC product
  • adonis/faq-accordion — native <details> · ACF repeater
  • adonis/disclaimer · adonis/final-cta

Package CTA → cart WC standard

  • "Start Precision Care" links to ?add-to-cart=<product_id> — native WC URL
  • Cart page is the same shared cart we already designed; line item shows package name + included components in woocommerce_get_item_data
  • Single line, single quantity (locked) — Precision Care is a one-time package
  • Tax: WC tax class "service" · 6% FL state, no county for digital service
  • Shipping: virtual — no shipping charge; phlebotomy kit logistics handled by Prescribery / lab partner

Charge timing Stripe authorize-only

  • Payment Intent capture_method: manual at checkout — same pattern as Express
  • Order status: on-hold after checkout
  • Member services confirms eligibility + schedules visit → manual capture via WC admin → status processing
  • If declined eligibility: cancel intent → status refunded → email user
  • Copy on every CTA must reflect this: "Card authorized · captured after member-services confirmation"

Prescribery handoff after capture

  • WC order_status_processing hook → POST to Prescribery /precision-cases
  • Returns intake_url, case_id, visit_calendar_url
  • Thank-you page → "Continue to your medical questionnaire" → opens Prescribery intake
  • Webhooks: case.intake_complete, case.lab_drawn, case.visit_scheduled, case.visit_complete, case.plan_delivered — each updates order meta + emails member
  • Adonis stores order timeline; the clinical record stays in Prescribery

Lab partner logistics, not record

  • At-home phlebotomy: Getlabs or equivalent — Prescribery dispatches
  • In-network draw: LabCorp or Quest requisition emailed by Prescribery
  • Adonis does not see raw lab values — they go to the physician via Prescribery's clinical layer
  • The diagnostic visualization on the page is marketing illustration, not a live patient view — fed from a static JSON
  • "~50 markers" is the marketing figure; the physician selects the actual panel per case

Compliance copy required & banned

  • Required on every Rx-adjacent CTA: provider-review reassurance line
  • Required in disclaimer: "Prescriptions or therapies are provided only when medically appropriate…"
  • Banned: "guaranteed prescription", "guaranteed results", "instant", "cure", "treats X" without qualifier
  • Banned: claims a specific biomarker means a specific diagnosis — biomarker section must be framed as "data behind the visit", not diagnosis
  • State licensure + 503A disclosure required in footer + disclaimer

FAQ do not generate

  • FAQ copy is hand-written and legally reviewed — no AI generation, no auto-summaries
  • Stored as ACF repeater on the page itself (not a global FAQ CPT) so legal can review per-page
  • Specific bans: never claim Express is "less safe", never imply Precision is required for Rx, never state outcome guarantees
  • "Does this guarantee a prescription?" must always answer "No." — first word, bolded

Do-not-build defer to Prescribery

  • No custom intake form — Prescribery owns it (HIPAA + BAA covered)
  • No custom video visit tooling — Prescribery integrates with telehealth provider (Doxy.me, Zoom Health)
  • No custom physician dashboard — Prescribery owns the clinician UI
  • No custom lab record viewer for members — they receive the written plan PDF; raw labs available on request through member services
  • No custom Rx record / dispense log — pharmacy partner + Prescribery

Member portal minimum viable

  • WP frontend at /member · WooCommerce My Account screens, restyled
  • Tabs: Orders (WC native) · Care timeline (custom · reads order meta from Prescribery webhooks) · Documents (plan PDF link from Prescribery) · Subscriptions (treatment Rx, native WC Subs)
  • No clinical decision-making, no lab dashboards, no AI insights
  • Communication preferences: email + SMS toggles (Prescribery sends, we render preference)

Design tokens shared with Express

  • Same v4.5 dark-luxury system — no Precision-only color
  • What signals "premium" here: typographic restraint (Fraunces italic for emphasis only), the diagnostic panel visual, the deep-green clinical reassurance accent, more whitespace, fewer CTAs per screen
  • The pricing card has the only gold-bordered surface on the page — single visual peak, intentional
  • Typography scale is one step larger than Express on h1/h2 — Precision feels editorial, Express feels operational
  • Shared partials: parts/notice-strip · parts/footer · parts/disclaimer · parts/reassure
