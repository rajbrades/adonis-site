03 · Who it's for

For the data-curious, the optimizing, and the genuinely concerned.

Some men come in already knowing what they want measured. Others come in because something feels off and a 30-second questionnaire isn't enough. Both are right at home in Precision Care.

01
Low energy that won't lift
Persistent fatigue, brain fog, or lost drive — even with reasonable sleep and exercise. Often thyroid, testosterone, iron, or insulin-driven when the root cause is biological.
02
Hair loss with broader context
Pattern hair loss can have hormonal and metabolic drivers worth checking — DHT, ferritin, thyroid, inflammation. Useful before or alongside a topical/oral protocol.
03
Sexual performance concerns
When ED isn't responsive to first-line treatment, or when libido has dropped — the answer is often hormonal, vascular, or metabolic. Worth knowing which.
04
Recovery and training issues
Slower recovery, persistent injuries, plateaus you can't out-train. We measure inflammation, recovery hormones, and the metabolic markers your wearable can't.
05
Hormone concerns
Total and free testosterone, SHBG, estradiol, DHT, prolactin, cortisol. Read together — not in isolation.
06
Thyroid concerns
Beyond TSH alone — Free T3, Free T4, reverse T3, antibodies. Where the GP often stops, we begin.
07
Longevity goals
Cardiometabolic risk read deeper than your annual physical: ApoB, Lp(a), insulin, hs-CRP, HbA1c. The markers that actually move with intervention.
08

09 · Common questions

Things worth asking.

If you don't see your question here, member services replies the same business day during launch. We answer in plain language, not legalese.

Q · 01Is Precision Care required for treatment?

No. Express Care is the right path for most members seeking treatment for pattern hair loss or ED. Precision Care is for members who want lab-level context — hormones, thyroid, metabolic markers — alongside, before, or instead of treatment.

Many members start with Express Care, see good results, and only move to Precision Care if questions remain or new concerns arise.

Q · 02Can I start with Express Care first?

Yes. Express Care is a separate pathway with its own questionnaire, provider review, and treatment options. You can move to Precision Care at any time — it's the deeper diagnostic complement, not a prerequisite or a replacement.

Q · 03What labs are included?

The standard Precision Care panel covers ~50 biomarkers across six categories: hormones (testosterone total + free, SHBG, estradiol, DHT, prolactin, cortisol), full thyroid, metabolic / insulin axis, inflammation, nutrients, and cardiometabolic risk (ApoB, Lp(a), particle sizing).

Your physician may request additional or alternate markers based on your intake. Specific markers and reference ranges depend on your case.

Q · 04What happens after I purchase?

Your card is authorized but not charged in full at checkout. A member-services coordinator confirms your eligibility, schedules your physician visit, and ships your phlebotomy kit (or schedules a LabCorp draw). Charge is captured at that confirmation. No charge if you don't qualify clinically.

Q · 05Can I use outside labs?

If you've had compatible labs run within the last 90 days, you can submit them and we'll discount the package by the lab portion (typically $150). Your physician decides whether the panels are sufficient or if any markers need to be re-drawn.

Q · 06Does this guarantee a prescription?

No. Precision Care provides medical evaluation and personalized recommendations. A prescription is issued only when medically appropriate, by a licensed physician, after reviewing your labs, intake, and visit. No reputable medical practice can guarantee a prescription, and we don't.

Q · 07Is this available for men and women?

Adonis is currently focused on men's performance medicine — hair, sexual wellness, hormone, thyroid, and metabolic optimization. A women's pathway with its own panels, plans, and physicians is in active development; join the early access list from the women's home view.
