04 · Product education

What this protocol actually does.

Read before you check out — these are the things our physicians want every member to understand before starting.

Who it's for

Best candidates

Men with mild-to-moderate androgenetic alopecia (Norwood I–IV) who have at least 18 months of available follicles.

  • Visible miniaturization at the crown or temples
  • No history of finasteride sensitivity
  • Realistic expectation of slowed loss + partial regrowth
How it works

Mechanism, plainly

Finasteride blocks DHT conversion at the follicle; minoxidil extends anagen phase and increases follicular blood flow.

  • 5α-reductase type II inhibition (oral)
  • Vasodilation + K+ channel opening (topical)
  • Complementary pathways, additive in trial data
What to expect

Realistic timeline

Initial shedding is common in months 1–3. Visible density changes typically begin around month 6 with continued use.

  • Months 1–3 — shedding phase, expected
  • Months 4–6 — early density stabilization
  • Months 6–12 — measurable regrowth in responders
Safety considerations

Known tradeoffs

The molecules are well-characterized; their side-effect profile is too. Your physician will discuss these specifically.

  • Possible sexual side effects (~2–4% in trials)
  • Topical irritation or scalp dryness
  • Not for use during pregnancy or nursing
When this isn't right

Decline criteria

Adonis providers will decline approval when this protocol is not appropriate. We'd rather say no than ship the wrong thing.

  • Pregnancy, nursing, or planning conception
  • History of finasteride-related side effects
  • Non-androgenic causes (alopecia areata, scarring, telogen)

05 · Before you check out

Direct answers, before you order.

How does provider review actually work?
After checkout you'll complete a medical questionnaire built for this specific protocol. A Florida-licensed physician reads your answers, your medication list, and any flags — then either approves, requests follow-ups, modifies the prescription, or declines. You are never auto-prescribed. Average review time is 18 hours; expedited review is available.
What happens immediately after checkout?
You'll receive an email with a link to your medical questionnaire and your member portal. We hold the full charge until your provider has cleared the case. If your case is declined, your card is refunded automatically.
What if I'm not approved?
If a provider determines the protocol isn't appropriate for you, you'll receive a written explanation and a full refund. In some cases the provider will suggest an alternative protocol or recommend the Precision Care path. There is no charge for a declined case.
How do subscriptions work?
Subscriptions ship on the cadence you select (monthly or bi-monthly). You can pause, modify dose, or cancel from your member portal — no call, no retention pitch. Subscription members are eligible for quarterly check-ins with their provider, included.
When does the medication ship?
Medication ships only after provider approval. Typical timeline is 3–5 business days from approval to your door for generics, 5–7 for compounded protocols. All shipments are unbranded with tracking and signature on request.
Are results guaranteed?
No. No reputable medical practice can guarantee a clinical outcome, and we won't either. Response varies based on biology, genetics, baseline severity, and adherence. We track outcomes longitudinally and adjust your protocol when the data calls for it.
