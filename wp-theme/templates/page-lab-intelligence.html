05 · About the AI

Pattern recognition, not a diagnosis.

The intelligence in Lab Intelligence is in how the data is organized, summarized, and made legible — not in any attempt to replace your physician. We're explicit about both sides.

What it does

Organize. Summarize.
Help you ask better questions.

  • AI-powered organization of your lab data — sorting markers across hormones, thyroid, metabolic, inflammation, nutrients, and cardiometabolic categories.
  • Pattern recognition across panels — flags markers that have moved meaningfully between draws, or that sit outside reference ranges.
  • Educational summaries in plain English — what each marker generally indicates, where reference ranges come from, and why a physician might want to look closer.
  • Helps you prepare better questions for your provider. Saves a one-page discussion summary you can share with any physician — yours, ours, or another.
Educational use. Reviewed against published reference ranges. Not personalized medical advice.
What it does not do

Doesn't diagnose. Doesn't
prescribe. Doesn't replace.

  • Does not diagnose — no condition labels, no "you have X." Reference comparisons only, with the explicit framing that diagnosis requires a licensed provider.
  • Does not prescribe — no medication recommendations, no dosing, no protocols. Prescriptions are issued only by a licensed provider via Precision Care.
  • Does not deliver a treatment plan — pattern flags are educational. A clinical plan requires intake, history, and physician judgment.
  • Does not replace your physician — and we don't market it as one. No "AI doctor", no "AI treatment plan", no guarantees of optimization. Your provider is your provider.
Banned framings. AI doctor · AI diagnosis · AI prescription · AI treatment plan · guaranteed optimization. Not used here, not used in marketing.

09 · Common questions

Things worth asking.

If you don't see your question here, member services replies the same business day during launch. We answer in plain language.

Q · 01Can I upload outside labs?

Yes. PDFs, photos, and CSV exports from Quest, LabCorp, your last physical, or other CLIA-certified labs are all supported. We organize the data; the original file remains yours and is downloadable from your member portal at any time.

Q · 02Can I order labs through Adonis?

Yes — single-category panels start at $199, full panels at $349. We use the same Quest / LabCorp partner network as Precision Care, with at-home phlebotomy as an option. Lab spend credits toward the $499 Precision Care package if you upgrade within 90 days.

Q · 03Is this medical advice?

No. Lab Intelligence is for educational review and organization of health data. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. For clinical interpretation and personalized medical guidance, upgrade to Precision Care or share your insights summary with your existing provider.

Q · 04Can AI prescribe treatment?

No. No AI on this platform — or any other reputable platform — can prescribe medications. Prescriptions are issued only by a licensed physician, only after reviewing your intake, history, and current presentation, only when medically appropriate. We're explicit about this throughout the product.

Q · 05When should I upgrade to Precision Care?

Upgrade when you want a physician's interpretation of your data — whether because something on your panel concerns you, because you have symptoms you'd like correlated to your bloodwork, or because you want a written plan. Lab Intelligence is the on-ramp; Precision Care is where labs become a plan.

Members commonly upgrade after seeing 1–2 flagged markers, or after a follow-up draw shows movement they'd like to discuss with a clinician.

Q · 06Are my labs secure?

Documents are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), stored in a HIPAA-compliant environment, accessible only to you. We don't sell, share, or use your data for advertising. You can request deletion of any uploaded document at any time from the member portal.

Q · 07Can I use this for hormones, thyroid, nutrients, and longevity?

Yes — those are exactly the categories Lab Intelligence is built around. The six default categories are hormones, thyroid, metabolic, inflammation, nutrients, and cardiometabolic risk. If your panel includes markers in these areas, they'll be organized and summarized educationally. Clinical interpretation still requires Precision Care.
